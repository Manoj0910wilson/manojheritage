Export process

From first message
to your port.

A clear, step-by-step way of working, so importers know what happens next at every stage.

  1. 01

    Tell us what you need

    Share the size or grade, colour, aroma, quantity, packing and destination. A short message is enough to begin.

  2. 02

    Approve a sample

    We send a sample so you can compare it with your specification. Agree what it represents before anything is committed.

  3. 03

    Receive a quotation

    We respond with a quotation based on size, quantity, packing, destination and delivery terms.

  4. 04

    Confirm the order

    Agree the quantity, packing, delivery terms and timing, including any seasonal peak you are planning for.

  5. 05

    Packing and documents

    We prepare and pack your cardamom and arrange the shipping documents your destination needs, working with your clearing agent.

  6. 06

    Shipment and delivery

    Your cardamom travels to the agreed port, usually by sea, with air freight available for small or urgent lots.

Delivery terms we can discuss

FOB (Free on Board)
We deliver the goods to the vessel at the Indian port. You arrange and pay for freight and insurance.
CFR (Cost and Freight)
We pay the sea freight to your destination port. You arrange insurance and import clearance.
CIF (Cost, Insurance and Freight)
As CFR, with marine insurance arranged to the destination port.

Documents

Shipments typically involve a commercial invoice, a packing list, a bill of lading or airway bill and a certificate of origin, plus any other certificates your destination requires. Rules differ by country and can change, so we confirm the exact list with you and your clearing agent for each order.

Packing

Cardamom is packed in food-grade packaging. We agree the packing size with you based on your storage, turnover and market.

Prices

Prices depend on size, quantity, packing, destination and delivery terms, so we quote on request instead of publishing a list.
