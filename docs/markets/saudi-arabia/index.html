A coffee culture built around cardamom

Saudi coffee, often called qahwa, is traditionally flavoured with cardamom, and serving it is central to hospitality in homes, offices and gatherings. That daily demand makes green cardamom one of the most important spices for Saudi importers.

Because the pod is part of the drink, buyers pay close attention to aroma. Pods with a fresh, sweet and clean scent are what coffee blenders and traders look for.

Where buyers are based

Trading activity is spread across the country. Riyadh is the large central market, Jeddah is the main gateway on the Red Sea coast, and Dammam and the Eastern Province connect to the Gulf. Importers in each region may have different preferences on size and packing, so we ask early about your market and your customers.

Shipping and port access

Sea freight to Jeddah Islamic Port or King Abdulaziz Port in Dammam is the usual route for container and pallet quantities. We are happy to discuss FOB, CFR or CIF terms.

Demand for cardamom often rises around Ramadan and the Hajj and Umrah seasons, when hospitality is at its busiest. If your business is seasonal, tell us your target timing so planning can start early.

Rules and documents

Food imports into Saudi Arabia are regulated, and requirements, including labelling, can change. Importers normally confirm the current rules with their customs agent. From our side we share product information and the documents your destination needs, and we are happy to work with your agent from the first enquiry.

Questions from Saudi Arabia buyers

Is Saudi coffee really made with cardamom?

Yes. Cardamom is a traditional ingredient in Saudi qahwa, which is why consistent aroma matters so much to buyers here.

Can you help with labelling and documents?

We share product details and the shipping documents your destination requires. Your clearing agent will confirm the latest import rules, and we work with them.

Can I order for a seasonal peak such as Ramadan?

Please tell us your timing as early as possible so we can plan supply and shipping around it.
