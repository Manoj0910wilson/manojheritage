Why the Gulf

Across the Arabian Peninsula, cardamom is part of daily hospitality, from the cup of Arabic coffee offered to every guest to the sweets served at celebrations. That steady, culture-driven demand makes the region one of the world’s most important markets for green cardamom, and aroma is what buyers care about most.
