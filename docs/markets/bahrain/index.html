Market · Bahrain

Green cardamom for
Bahrain.

Aromatic whole green cardamom from Idukki, Kerala, for Bahrain’s importers, sweet makers, cafés and food businesses.

Small market, high standards

Bahrain is compact, with a long tradition of trade and a food culture that takes spices seriously. Buyers here often work in smaller, regular lots and notice differences in freshness and aroma quickly.

Where cardamom shows up

Cardamom flavours Bahraini coffee and tea, and it is a key ingredient in Bahraini halwa and other traditional sweets. Cafés and bakeries also use it in modern recipes, from cakes to spiced drinks.

Shipping to Bahrain

Shipments commonly arrive through Khalifa Bin Salman Port, and some buyers also receive goods overland or via nearby Gulf ports. Smaller lots can travel by air. We are happy to discuss FOB, CFR or CIF terms and packing sizes that suit smaller, regular orders.
