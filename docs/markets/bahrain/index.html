Market · Bahrain

Green cardamom for
Bahrain.

Aromatic whole green cardamom from Idukki, Kerala, for Bahrain’s importers, sweet makers, cafés and food businesses.

Small market, high standards

Bahrain is compact, with a long tradition of trade and a food culture that takes spices seriously. Buyers here often work in smaller, regular lots and notice differences in freshness and aroma quickly.

Where cardamom shows up

Cardamom flavours Bahraini coffee and tea, and it is a key ingredient in Bahraini halwa and other traditional sweets. Cafés and bakeries also use it in modern recipes, from cakes to spiced drinks.

Shipping to Bahrain

Shipments commonly arrive through Khalifa Bin Salman Port, and some buyers also receive goods overland or via nearby Gulf ports. Smaller lots can travel by air. We are happy to discuss FOB, CFR or CIF terms and packing sizes that suit smaller, regular orders.

What to include in your request

To get a useful reply, tell us the size you need in millimetres, the colour and aroma you expect, the quantity, packing preference and delivery terms such as FOB, CFR or CIF. Our green cardamom buyer’s guide explains each of these points in plain terms.

Samples before you commit

We offer samples on request so you can check colour, aroma and cleanliness yourself before ordering. Tell us how you will use the cardamom, whether Arabic coffee, halwa and sweets, or catering, and we will suggest what suits.

Talk to us

If you are an importer, distributor or producer in Bahrain, send us your requirements on the sample request page. We will reply with how a sample and a first order could work.

Questions from Bahrain buyers

Can you supply smaller regular orders?

Tell us the quantity and frequency you have in mind, and we will discuss what is practical.

Do you support cafés and bakeries?

Many of our conversations are with importers, but we are glad to hear from food businesses too. Share what you need and we will say how we can help.
