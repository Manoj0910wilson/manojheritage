Why the UAE matters for cardamom

Dubai is one of the busiest trading and re-export hubs in the world, and the UAE spice trade has long served buyers well beyond its own borders. A single importer here may supply local retailers, hospitality groups and customers in neighbouring markets at the same time.

That makes consistency important. A buyer who re-exports or repacks needs pods that look and smell the same from one shipment to the next, so the conversation usually starts with clear specifications and a sample.

Who buys, and what for

Typical buyers include spice wholesalers and traders, coffee roasters, hotel and catering groups, bakeries and sweet makers, and food manufacturers that blend or pack their own products.

In the Emirates, cardamom-scented Arabic coffee is part of everyday hospitality, and cardamom also flavours tea, sweets and rice dishes. These uses reward a fresh, clean and sweet aroma, which is why we focus on whole green pods selected for that character.

Shipping from Kerala to the UAE

Cardamom for the UAE is usually shipped by sea to Jebel Ali or another UAE port, which suits container and pallet quantities. Air freight can make sense for small trial lots or urgent top-ups. We are happy to discuss FOB, CFR or CIF terms, depending on how you prefer to arrange freight.

Many Dubai importers operate from free zones or have a separate clearing agent. Tell us your consignee and agent details early, and we will confirm what paperwork your shipment needs.

What UAE buyers usually ask us for

Most first conversations cover the size or grade, colour, aroma, packing size and labelling, the documents that will accompany the shipment, and a sample to approve before a first order. You can send all of that in one message through our sample request page.
