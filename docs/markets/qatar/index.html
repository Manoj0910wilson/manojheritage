Carefully selected whole green cardamom from Idukki for Qatar’s importers, hospitality groups and specialty food businesses.

A quality-led market

Qatar is a smaller market than some of its neighbours, but it is a demanding one. Hotels, restaurant groups, catering companies and specialty cafés place a high value on consistent, good-looking, fragrant ingredients, and many prefer dependable supply in manageable lots over very large volumes.

How cardamom is used

Cardamom-scented Arabic coffee is a sign of welcome in Qatar, and cardamom is also used in tea, desserts and rice dishes. A growing café scene has added new uses too, from cardamom lattes to spiced baking, where a clean aroma shows through clearly.

Getting it to Doha

Shipments to Qatar commonly arrive through Hamad Port, and air freight is an option for small or urgent quantities. We are happy to discuss FOB, CFR or CIF terms, and to talk through packing sizes that suit your storage and turnover.

Starting a relationship

If you are a Qatar-based importer or buyer, the simplest first step is a sample. Send us your requirements on the sample request page, and we will reply with how we can work together.

Questions from Qatar buyers

Do you only supply very large volumes?

We are glad to discuss what quantity suits your business. Share your expected volume in your enquiry and we will respond with the options.

Can you supply hotels and catering companies directly?

Most of our conversations are with importers and distributors. If you are a hospitality group buying directly, tell us your needs and we will say how we can help.
