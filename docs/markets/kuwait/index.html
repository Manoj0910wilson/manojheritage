Fragrant whole green cardamom from Idukki, Kerala, for Kuwait’s importers, wholesalers, bakeries and beverage businesses.

Coffee, tea and sweets

Kuwaiti hospitality revolves around coffee and tea, and cardamom is a favourite in both. It also flavours traditional sweets, pastries and rice dishes, so demand comes from households, cafés, bakeries and food producers alike.

Who we talk to in Kuwait

Our conversations here are usually with spice importers and wholesalers, packers who sell under their own labels, bakery and confectionery producers, and coffee and tea blenders. Each has slightly different needs on size, packing and volume, so we start by listening to yours.

Shipping to Kuwait

Sea freight to Shuwaikh Port or Shuaiba Port is the usual route for larger quantities, with air freight possible for small samples and urgent lots. We are happy to discuss FOB, CFR or CIF terms.

Your clearing agent can confirm current import and labelling requirements for Kuwait. We share product information and shipping documents and are happy to coordinate with them.

Next step

Send us what you are looking for through the sample request page, or message us on WhatsApp. A written specification and a sample make the first order far smoother.

Questions from Kuwait buyers

Can I buy cardamom for my own brand and packing?

Yes, packers and private-label buyers are an important group. Tell us your preferred packing and we will discuss what is possible.
