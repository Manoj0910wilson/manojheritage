Kahwa and halwa

Omani kahwa is traditionally spiced with cardamom, and Omani halwa, the rich, slow-cooked sweet served at celebrations, is also scented with it. These two traditions give cardamom a special place in Omani homes and in the businesses that serve them.

Because both rely on aroma, buyers value pods that are fresh, fragrant and evenly selected.

A strong link with India

India and Oman have traded across the Arabian Sea for centuries, and the sea route is short compared with many other markets. That shared history is one reason Indian spices are so familiar to Omani buyers.

Ports and logistics

Shipments to Oman can arrive through ports such as Sohar, Salalah or Muscat, depending on where your business and customers are. We are happy to discuss FOB, CFR or CIF terms and to plan packing for your storage and distribution.
