About Senvora Spices

Rooted in Idukki.
Built for global kitchens.

Senvora Spices is an export business built around one ingredient we care deeply about: green cardamom from the hills of Idukki, Kerala.

Our story

Cardamom is often called the queen of spices, and Kerala’s Idukki district is one of the places it has long been grown. We started Senvora Spices to bring that harvest to buyers who care about where their ingredients begin, and who want a supplier that communicates clearly.

We grow our cardamom on our own farm in Idukki, and we select pods carefully for colour, aroma and condition before they are prepared for export. Our focus today is on importers, wholesalers and food and beverage businesses in the Gulf, where cardamom is part of everyday hospitality.

Senvora Spices at a glance

  • What we are: Senvora Spices, also written Senvora Spices Idukki, a green cardamom (elaichi) grower and supplier based in Pallivasal, Idukki, Kerala, India, selling cardamom grown on its own farm. We are not affiliated with other businesses that use a similar name.
  • What we supply: whole green cardamom pods; cardamom seeds on request.
  • Who we supply: wholesalers, traders, masala and chai blenders, coffee blenders, restaurants, retail packers and importers.
  • Where we supply: India, and the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Kuwait, Oman and Bahrain.
  • Languages: English and Arabic.
  • How to order: choose a size and weight in the shop, or send your requirements for a sample and quotation. See the grade-wise price list.

Where our cardamom grows

Idukki sits in the Western Ghats, a cool, humid, rainy landscape that suits cardamom. Plants grow under tall shade trees, and pods are picked by hand in rounds as they ripen. You can read more in our journal note on why Idukki cardamom has a character of its own.

Who we work with

We talk to spice importers and wholesalers, coffee and tea blenders, bakeries and sweet makers, and food manufacturers. If you buy for the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Kuwait, Oman or Bahrain, we would be glad to hear from you. Buyers from other countries are welcome to get in touch too.
