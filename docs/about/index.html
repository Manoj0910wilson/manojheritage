About Heritage Spices

Rooted in Idukki.
Built for global kitchens.

Heritage Spices is an export business built around one ingredient we care deeply about: green cardamom from the hills of Idukki, Kerala.

Our story

Cardamom is often called the queen of spices, and Kerala’s Idukki district is one of the places it has long been grown. We started Heritage Spices to bring that harvest to buyers who care about where their ingredients begin, and who want a supplier that communicates clearly.

We work directly with farmers in Idukki, and we select pods carefully for colour, aroma and condition before they are prepared for export. Our focus today is on importers, wholesalers and food and beverage businesses in the Gulf, where cardamom is part of everyday hospitality.

What we stand for

01

A traceable origin

We source directly from growers in Idukki, so we can talk about where your cardamom came from and who grew it.

02

Careful selection

Pods are chosen for a clean aroma, even colour and good condition, because small differences are easy to taste.

03

Clear communication

Written specifications, samples before orders, and straight answers on availability, packing and paperwork.

Where our cardamom grows

Idukki sits in the Western Ghats, a cool, humid, rainy landscape that suits cardamom. Plants grow under tall shade trees, and pods are picked by hand in rounds as they ripen. You can read more in our journal note on why Idukki cardamom has a character of its own.

Who we work with

We talk to spice importers and wholesalers, coffee and tea blenders, bakeries and sweet makers, and food manufacturers. If you buy for the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Kuwait, Oman or Bahrain, we would be glad to hear from you. Buyers from other countries are welcome to get in touch too.
