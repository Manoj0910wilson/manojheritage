Guide · Green cardamom

Green cardamom (elaichi):
the buyer’s guide.

Quick answer: green cardamom is the dried fruit pod of Elettaria cardamomum, a spice grown in warm, humid hills such as Idukki in Kerala and prized for its sweet, floral aroma in tea, coffee, sweets and rice dishes.

What is green cardamom?

Green cardamom is a spice made from the small, oval seed pods of a plant in the ginger family. Inside each pod are tiny dark seeds that carry the aroma. It is often called the “queen of spices” because of its fragrance and value.

It is the cardamom used in chai, Arabic coffee, kheer, biryani and many sweets. It is a different spice from black cardamom, which is larger and smoky. Read the differences between green and black cardamom.

Where it grows

Green cardamom needs warm, humid, shaded hill country with steady rain. In India the best-known growing areas are in the Western Ghats, including Idukki in Kerala. Guatemala is also a major source for world markets, and our origin comparison explains what importers should look at.

How green cardamom is graded

Pods are sorted mainly by size and colour. Indian trade names such as AGEB, AGB and AGS describe extra bold, bold and superior grades, and millimetre ranges vary by trader. See Indian cardamom grades explained and green cardamom sizes explained.

How it is used

Green cardamom flavours Arabic coffee, tea and chai, sweets, desserts and rice dishes. Read about cardamom in Arabic coffee and how blenders choose it in chai and masala blends.

How to buy it

Judge colour, aroma, size and cleanliness, and always buy against a sample. Our checklists cover buying in bulk in India and choosing a reliable supplier.

How to store it

Keep whole pods airtight, cool, dry and away from light and strong smells, and grind only what you need. See how to store green cardamom.

Common questions about green cardamom

What is green cardamom used for?

It flavours tea, Arabic coffee, sweets, desserts, baking and rice dishes such as biryani and pulao.

Is green cardamom the same as elaichi?

Yes. Elaichi is the Hindi name for cardamom, and in everyday use it usually means green cardamom.

What is the difference between green and black cardamom?

Green cardamom is small, sweet and floral. Black cardamom is larger, smoky and used mainly in savoury dishes. They are different spices.

How is green cardamom graded?

Mainly by pod size and colour, from extra bold to bold and smaller grades. Size ranges vary by trader, so ask for millimetres and a sample.

How should I store green cardamom?

Whole pods in an airtight container in a cool, dry, dark place, away from strong smells. Grind only what you need.
