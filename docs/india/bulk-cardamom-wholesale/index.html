India · Bulk and wholesale

Bulk cardamom for
wholesale buyers.

Planning regular volumes of green cardamom? We work with Indian wholesalers, traders and manufacturers on grade, packing and repeat supply.

What to tell us in your first message

The fastest route to a useful quotation is a short message covering the size or grade you want, the monthly or one-time quantity, your delivery city, and whether you pack under your own brand.

Send it through the sample request page or on WhatsApp and we will respond with options. Our bulk buying checklist explains what to check before you order.

Grades, colour and size

Buyers usually judge cardamom on pod size, green colour, aroma and the share of broken or faded pods. We select for bright, aromatic pods and confirm the grade and size available on enquiry rather than promising what we cannot document.

Repeat supply and planning

If you buy every month, tell us. Agreeing quantities in advance helps us plan sourcing from Idukki, especially ahead of the festival and wedding seasons.

Questions from Indian buyers

What is the minimum order for bulk supply?

It depends on grade, size and packing. Tell us your quantity and we will respond with options.

Can you pack under our brand?

Tell us what you need and we will say what is possible.
