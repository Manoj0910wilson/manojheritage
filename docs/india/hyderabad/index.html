Cardamom for Hyderabad biryani, chai and sweets

Hyderabad cooks with cardamom in biryani and other rice dishes, in Irani chai and everyday tea, and in many sweets. Whole green pods added at the right moment give a clear, sweet aroma that a stale or powdered spice cannot.

We grow our cardamom on our own farm in Idukki, Kerala, and select it by size. Choose 8 mm pods for the best look and aroma, or 7 mm and 6 mm for everyday cooking and blends.

Choose by size, see the price today

We sell whole green cardamom by pod size: 8 mm for premium use, 7 mm for everyday cooking and chai, 6 mm for blends and 5 mm for grinding. Larger pods look and smell better and cost more per kilo.

Retail packs run from 25 g to 10 kg and the rate per kilo falls a little on larger packs. See today’s rates on the price list, then pick a size and weight in our shop and order on WhatsApp.

Who buys from us in Hyderabad

Home cooks, biryani and catering businesses, sweet shops, chai and café owners, and resellers who repack. For resale and large kitchens, ask for wholesale rates from 25 kg.

New to buying by size? Our guide to cardamom sizes explains 8 mm, 7 mm and smaller pods.
