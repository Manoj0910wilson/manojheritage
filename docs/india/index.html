Idukki green cardamom
for buyers across India.

Whole green cardamom (elaichi) from the hills of Idukki, Kerala, for wholesalers, masala and tea blenders, sweet makers, hotels and food businesses in every Indian state.

Why buy cardamom direct from Idukki

Idukki is the heart of Kerala’s cardamom country. Buying close to the source means fewer hands between the farm and your warehouse, a clearer story about where your cardamom was grown, and a conversation directly with the people who select it.

For many Indian buyers the real questions are colour, aroma, size and consistency from lot to lot. We start with those, share what we can confirm, and send a sample before you commit.

Who we supply in India

Our conversations are with spice wholesalers and traders, masala and chai blenders, packers and private-label brands, mithai and bakery businesses, hotels, caterers and restaurants, and food manufacturers.

Elaichi is part of daily life here: in chai, in biryani and pulao, in kheer, halwa and festival sweets. These uses reward a fresh, sweet and strongly aromatic pod, which is the character we select for.

How ordering works within India

Tell us the size or grade you need, your quantity and your city. We reply with options, a sample and a quotation. Goods move by road, rail or courier depending on the quantity and your location, and we confirm packing and billing details with you for each order.

Regular buyers can discuss repeat supply so that stock is planned ahead of festivals and wedding seasons, when demand rises. Read our green cardamom specification page for what to ask us, and our guide on how to buy green cardamom in bulk.

Looking at export too?

The same cardamom is available to importers abroad. See our pages for the UAE, Saudi Arabia and the wider Gulf.

Questions from Indian buyers

Yes. India is one of our main markets. We supply wholesalers, blenders, sweet makers, hotels and food businesses.

Yes. Send your requirements through the sample request page and we will tell you how a sample can reach you.

Do you supply small quantities?

Tell us the quantity you have in mind and we will say what is practical. Wholesale and bulk buyers are our main focus.
