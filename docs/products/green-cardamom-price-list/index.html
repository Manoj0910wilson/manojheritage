Frequently asked questions

How are your cardamom prices set?

Prices follow the daily Spices Board of India small cardamom auction, adjusted for grade, with our margin on top. They update every day.

What do AGEB, AGB and AGS mean?

They are Alleppey Green grades: Extra Bold, Bold and Superior. Larger, greener, more uniform pods sit in higher grades. See grades explained.

What is the minimum for wholesale prices?

Wholesale prices apply from 25 kg. For export or larger quantities, message us on WhatsApp for a quotation.

Is the price final?

Prices are indicative and follow the market. We confirm the final price, availability and delivery on WhatsApp before you pay.
