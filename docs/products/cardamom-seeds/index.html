Overview

Cardamom seeds are the small, dark seeds found inside the pod. They carry most of the aroma and are used where whole pods are not practical, for example in some spice blends, bakery recipes and beverage mixes.

If you are not sure which form suits you, we are glad to talk it through. Many buyers who make coffee blends choose whole green cardamom instead, because the pod helps protect the aroma.

Frequently asked questions

Why choose whole pods over seeds?

Seeds can lose aroma faster once they leave the pod, and whole pods are easier to inspect. Many buyers therefore prefer whole green cardamom. Seeds suit applications where pods are not needed.

Is the seed product always available?

Availability and specification are confirmed on enquiry. Send us your requirements and we will tell you what we can offer.
