Overview

Green cardamom (Elettaria cardamomum) is the whole dried pod of a plant grown in the shaded, rainy hills of the Western Ghats. Our pods come from Idukki, Kerala, and are sourced directly from farmers, then selected for colour, aroma and condition before export.

Buying whole pods keeps the aroma protected until you are ready to use it, and it lets you inspect size, colour and cleanliness yourself, which is why many Gulf importers and coffee blenders prefer them.

Common uses

Arabic coffee, tea and karak, sweets and halwa, bakery and pastry, rice dishes, spice blends and beverage mixes. Read more about cardamom in Arabic coffee.

Also known as

Green cardamom is called elaichi in Hindi, elakkai in Tamil, elakka in Malayalam and هيل (hayl) in Arabic. It is sometimes called the “queen of spices”.

How to choose and buy

Pod size, colour and aroma decide quality. Read how Indian cardamom grades (AGEB, AGB, AGS) work, the differences between green and black cardamom, our bulk buying checklist, and how to store cardamom so it keeps its aroma.

Who it is for

How should whole green cardamom be stored?

Keep it sealed, in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight and strong odours. Stored this way, whole pods keep their aroma far better than ground cardamom.

Is green cardamom the same as black cardamom?

No. They are different spices. Green cardamom is small, sweet and floral and is used in tea, coffee and sweets. Black cardamom is larger, smoky and used mainly in savoury cooking.
