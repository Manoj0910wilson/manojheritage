FAQ

Frequently asked
questions.

Quick answers for importers and buyers. If yours is not here, just ask.

Where does your cardamom come from?

Our green cardamom comes from Idukki, Kerala, in the Western Ghats of southern India, and is sourced directly from farmers.

Which products do you supply?

Our main product is whole green cardamom. Cardamom seeds are available on enquiry. See the products page.

Which countries do you supply?

Our focus is the Gulf: the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Kuwait, Oman and Bahrain. Buyers from other countries are welcome to enquire.

Can I get a sample before ordering?

Yes. Samples are available on request. Use the sample request page and tell us your requirements and destination.

What is your minimum order quantity?

Minimum quantities depend on size, packing and shipping method and are shared on enquiry. Tell us the volume you are considering and we will respond with options.

Do you publish prices?

No. Prices depend on size, quantity, packing, destination and delivery terms, so we quote on request.

Which delivery terms do you offer?

We are happy to discuss FOB, CFR and CIF. Read more on the export process page.

Which documents come with a shipment?

Typically a commercial invoice, packing list, bill of lading or airway bill and certificate of origin, plus any certificates your destination requires. We confirm the exact list with you for each order.

How should I store green cardamom?

Keep it sealed, in a cool, dry place, away from direct sunlight and strong odours.

How do I place an order?

Send us your requirements, approve a sample, receive a quotation and confirm the order. The full process is on the export process page.
