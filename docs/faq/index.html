FAQ

Frequently asked
questions.

Quick answers for importers and buyers. If yours is not here, just ask.

Where does your cardamom come from?

Our green cardamom comes from Idukki, Kerala, in the Western Ghats of southern India, and is grown on our own farm.

Which products do you supply?

Our main product is whole green cardamom. Cardamom seeds are available on enquiry. See the products page.

Which countries do you supply?

India is one of our main markets (see buying cardamom in India). We also supply importers in the Gulf: the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Kuwait, Oman and Bahrain. Buyers from other countries are welcome to enquire.

Can I get a sample before ordering?

Yes. Samples are available on request. Use the sample request page and tell us your requirements and destination.

What is your minimum order quantity?

Minimum quantities depend on size, packing and shipping method and are shared on enquiry. Tell us the volume you are considering and we will respond with options.

Do you publish prices?

Yes, for retail packs from 1 kg to 10 kg: see the Products page and order on WhatsApp. Wholesale and export prices depend on size, quantity, packing, destination and delivery terms, so we quote those on request.

Which delivery terms do you offer?

We are happy to discuss FOB, CFR and CIF. Read more on the export process page.

Which documents come with a shipment?

Typically a commercial invoice, packing list, bill of lading or airway bill and certificate of origin, plus any certificates your destination requires. We confirm the exact list with you for each order.

How should I store green cardamom?

Keep it sealed, in a cool, dry place, away from direct sunlight and strong odours.

Why is cardamom expensive?

It is grown in shaded hills and picked by hand in several rounds, then carefully dried and sorted. Prices also move with crop size, grade and demand. Read why cardamom is so expensive.

What is the difference between green and black cardamom?

Green cardamom is small, light and sweetly aromatic, and is used in coffee, tea, sweets and rice. Black cardamom is larger, dried over smoke and has a smoky flavour. See green vs black cardamom.

Are cardamom pods, seeds and powder the same?

They come from the same spice, but whole pods keep aroma longest while seeds and powder fade faster. See pods vs seeds vs powder.

How long does green cardamom last?

Whole pods stored sealed in a cool, dry place keep their aroma for many months. See how long green cardamom lasts.

When is cardamom harvested in Kerala?

Pods are picked by hand in rounds over several months, mainly in the later part of the year. See the Kerala harvest season.

What is elaichi?

Elaichi is the Hindi and Urdu name for cardamom. Heritage Spices supplies whole green elaichi from Idukki, Kerala. Read the complete guide.

How can I check cardamom quality at home?

Check colour, size, dryness, cleanliness, aroma and the seeds inside. See 6 simple quality tests.

What is cardamom called in Malayalam and Arabic?

It is elakka in Malayalam, elaichi in Hindi and hail (هيل) in Arabic. See cardamom names in other languages.

How do I place an order?

Send us your requirements, approve a sample, receive a quotation and confirm the order. The full process is on the export process page.
