How to choose a reliable cardamom supplier in Kerala

Quick answer: judge a cardamom supplier by sample quality, clear grade and packing details, written terms, registrations and how well they answer questions.

Cardamom is a high-value spice, so choosing the wrong supplier can be expensive. Whether you are a wholesaler in Mumbai or an importer in Dubai, these checks help you separate a dependable supplier from a risky one.

1. Test a sample first

A trustworthy supplier is happy to send a sample drawn from the lot you will actually receive. Judge colour, aroma, size and the share of broken or empty pods, and keep a sealed copy to compare against your delivery.

2. Get grade and packing in writing

Vague words such as “best quality” mean little. Ask for the size range in millimetres, the grade name, the packing type and the quantity per bag, and have them confirmed in writing before you pay.

3. Ask where the cardamom comes from

A good supplier can explain which region the pods come from and how they are selected and dried. Idukki in Kerala is one of India’s best-known growing areas, so ask how your supplier sources from there.

4. Check registrations and paperwork

Ask for business details, tax registration and, for export, the Importer-Exporter Code (IEC) and Spices Board registration. A supplier who dodges these questions is a warning sign. Senvora Spices does not yet list these registrations on this site and will add them once they are in place.

5. Compare prices carefully

A price far below the market for the same stated grade usually means a lower grade, older stock or a different weight. Compare quotations like for like: same grade, same packing, same delivery terms.

6. Look at how they communicate

Prompt, clear and honest answers, including about what they cannot supply, are a good sign. Start with a small order before committing to a large one.

7. Agree terms before shipping

Settle payment terms, delivery terms (FOB, CFR or CIF for export), packing and what happens if the lot does not match the sample.
