Cardamom price per kg: 8 mm vs 7 mm vs 6 mm

Quick answer: bigger, greener pods cost more per kg. 8 mm sits above the auction average, 7 mm near it and 6 mm below it.

Quick answer: green cardamom is priced per kg by pod size and quality. In general 8 mm (extra bold) costs the most, 7 mm (bold) is close to the day's auction average, and 6 mm and smaller cost less. Check the live rates on our cardamom price today page.

Why size changes the price

Large, deep-green, uniform pods are a small share of any harvest, and importers, roasters and gift buyers compete for them. Smaller pods are plentiful and are used where looks matter less, such as masala blends. Read green cardamom sizes explained for how the grades are sorted.

How the three main sizes compare

8 mm (AGEB): the boldest pods, chosen for Arabic coffee, gifting and premium retail. 7 mm (AGB/AGS): the best balance of price and quality for chai, sweets and restaurants. 6 mm: good aroma at a lower price, suited to blends, grinding and bulk processing.

Pack size matters too

The per-kg price of a 25 g pouch is higher than a 1 kg pack, and a 25 kg wholesale lot is cheaper again, because packing, labelling and handling are spread over fewer grams. See our retail packs or bulk wholesale.

How to choose

Buy 8 mm when appearance and gifting matter, 7 mm for everyday cooking and 6 mm for blends. Compare quotes on the same day, because auction prices move daily. To judge quality yourself, use how to check cardamom quality at home.

Quick answers

Why is 8 mm cardamom more expensive than 7 mm?

Pod size, deep green colour and uniformity are rarer in a harvest, so buyers pay more for 8 mm pods. The aroma can be excellent in smaller pods too, so choose by use, not size alone.

Which size is best for chai and sweets?

7 mm is the everyday choice: good colour and aroma at a lower price than 8 mm. 6 mm works well in masala blends and grinding.

Where can I see the live price for each size?

The Senvora Spices cardamom price today page lists retail and wholesale rates per kg for every size, refreshed every few hours.

Is the price per kg lower in bigger packs?

Yes. Larger packs and wholesale lots (25 kg and above) cost less per kg than 25 g to 500 g pouches, which carry more packing and handling per gram.
