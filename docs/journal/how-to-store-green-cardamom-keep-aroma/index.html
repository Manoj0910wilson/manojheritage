How to store green cardamom so it keeps its aroma

Quick answer: keep whole green cardamom pods in an airtight container, away from heat, light and moisture, and grind only what you need.

Cardamom is valued for its aroma, and aroma is exactly what is lost when it is stored badly. The good news is that the rules are simple, for a home kitchen and for a warehouse.

Keep it whole

The pod protects the seeds. Whole green pods hold their fragrance far longer than loose seeds or ground powder, so store and buy whole pods and crush them just before use.

Airtight, cool and dark

Use an airtight container such as a glass jar or a sealed food-grade bag, and keep it somewhere cool, dry and out of sunlight. Heat and light speed up the loss of aroma, and moisture can lead to mould.

Keep it away from strong smells

Spices absorb nearby odours. Do not store cardamom beside strong-smelling goods such as dried fish, chemicals or pungent spices.

For wholesalers and warehouses

Store bags off the floor, away from walls, in a dry and ventilated room. Check moisture before accepting stock, rotate older lots first and keep a sealed sample from each delivery for comparison. Our buying checklist explains what to look for when you receive a lot.
