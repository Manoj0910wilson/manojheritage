Cardamom pods vs seeds vs powder: which should you buy?

Quick answer: whole pods keep aroma longest; seeds and powder are convenient but lose fragrance faster.

Cardamom is sold in three common forms: whole pods, loose seeds and ground powder. Each suits a different use.

Whole green pods

The pod protects the seeds inside, so the aroma lasts longest. Whole pods are preferred for Arabic coffee, chai, rice dishes and for buyers who grind or repack themselves. Pod size, colour and uniformity are easy to judge by eye. See our whole green cardamom.

Cardamom seeds

Seeds are the small dark kernels inside the pod. They are handy when you do not want the husk, for example in some blends and sweets. Once out of the pod they lose aroma faster, so keep them sealed. See cardamom seeds.

Ground cardamom

Powder is the most convenient and the fastest to fade. Many cooks and blenders prefer to grind whole pods or seeds just before use for a stronger, fresher fragrance.

How aroma behaves in each form

Cardamom aroma comes from natural oils in the seeds. In a whole pod those oils stay sealed in. When seeds are removed or ground, more of the oil is exposed to air, so the fragrance fades faster. That is why many cooks, coffee makers and blenders choose pods and crush or grind them only when needed.

Which form suits which use

Arabic coffee and chai makers usually prefer whole pods. Bakers and some blenders may prefer seeds or powder for convenience. Restaurants often keep pods and grind small amounts as required. Retail packers often buy whole pods and repack. If you are unsure, ask for a sample and compare.

Which is best for bulk buyers?

Most importers and blenders start with whole pods because they are easier to grade, store and verify. Read our complete green cardamom guide for more, or send your requirements.
