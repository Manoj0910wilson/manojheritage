How to check green cardamom quality at home: 6 simple tests

Quick answer: judge colour, size, dryness, cleanliness, aroma and seeds. Good pods are green, even, dry, clean and strongly fragrant.

You do not need a laboratory to judge green cardamom. Six simple checks, done on a sample in good light, tell you a lot before you place an order.

1. Colour

Look for a bright, even green. Pale, yellowish or patchy pods may be older or poorly dried. Slight natural variation is normal, but a lot that is mostly faded is a warning sign.

2. Size and uniformity

Pods of similar size are easier to grade, pack and sell. Mixed sizes are fine if you are told in advance, but you should know what you are buying. See cardamom sizes explained.

3. Dryness

Pods should feel dry and firm, never damp or sticky. Excess moisture can cause mould and loss of aroma during storage and shipping.

4. Cleanliness

Check for stalks, dust, broken husk and foreign matter. A clean lot shows careful sorting.

5. Aroma

Crush one pod gently between your fingers. A good pod gives a strong, sweet, fresh fragrance straight away. A weak or musty smell suggests age or poor storage.

6. Seeds inside

Open a few pods. The seeds should be dark, plump and slightly sticky with natural oil, not pale, shrivelled or dusty.

Compare samples side by side

Always compare two or more samples in the same light, on the same day. For a full checklist read what to look for in a premium green pod, and see Indian grades. When you are ready, request a sample from Heritage Spices.
