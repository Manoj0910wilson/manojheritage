Choosing cardamom for chai and masala blends

Quick answer: chai and masala blenders usually value strong aroma and consistency over pod size, and should buy whole pods and grind close to use.

For a chai or masala brand, cardamom is often the most expensive and most noticeable ingredient. Choosing it well protects both your flavour and your margin.

Prioritise aroma and consistency

Customers taste your blend every time, so a steady aroma matters more than a showy pod. Smell samples from the lot, brew a test cup of tea and compare lots against an approved sample.

Size can be a cost lever

Very large pods look impressive and often cost more. If the pods will be ground into a blend, a smaller grade with the same aroma may serve you just as well. Ask for your options in our grade guide.

Buy whole, grind late

Whole pods keep their fragrance far longer than powder. Grind in batches close to production and store the rest properly, as explained in our storage guide.

Watch moisture and cleanliness

Damp or dusty stock can spoil and lower the quality of your blend. Check a sample for moisture, stalks, broken pods and dust before you commit to volume.

Supply planning

Plan ahead for festival and wedding seasons, when demand rises. See our bulk and wholesale page or request a sample with your monthly quantity.
